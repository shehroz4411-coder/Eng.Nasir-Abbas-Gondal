How a building goes up

The same six stages on every site, so you always know what is happening on your plot this week.

  1. Site visit

    We measure the plot, check the soil and access, and listen to what you want to build.

  2. Naqsha & estimate

    Plans, 3D elevation and a written estimate with materials and a timeline.

  3. Foundation

    Excavation, layout and foundation work, checked before anything goes on top.

  4. Grey structure

    Columns, beams, brickwork and slab casting, floor by floor.

  5. Finishing

    Plaster, electrical, plumbing, tiles, woodwork and paint.

  6. Handover

    A final walk-through together, and the keys are yours.
